## Stylesheet modules

- `tokens.css`: theme tokens and global transitions.
- `layout.css`: application shell, sidebar, header, and shared content surfaces.
- `catalogue.css`: product catalogue, shared controls, statistics, and work-in-progress card.
- `media-library.css`: File Browser, media cards, selection controls, and preview.
- `forms-and-dialogs.css`: product/media forms, upload controls, confirmations, and notifications.
- `responsive.css`: viewport-specific adjustments for all modules.

Keep `../style.css` as the single entry point loaded by the dashboard.
